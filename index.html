CHAPTER 01 · THE BEGINNING

Before you became my favourite person...

there was simply you.

07 November 2008.
The day the person who would one day become such an important part of my life came into this world.

Aur funny baat ye hai... us din mujhe bilkul idea nahi tha ki years later, main tumhara birthday itne pyaar se celebrate karunga.

01 THE DAY

07 November 2008.

Duniya ke liye ek normal date thi. Calendar mein ek aur number. Ek aur day.

But for me, looking at it today, it feels a little different.

Because that was the day you arrived here... long before either of us knew that someday our paths would cross.

And somehow, I'm really glad you did.

02 BEFORE US

You had a whole life before I knew you.

Tumhari apni childhood thi. Apni memories. Apne dreams. Apne favourite songs. Apni favourite places.

Aise moments bhi the jinke baare mein mujhe kuch nahi pata.

And honestly, I wish I could have seen every version of you.

The little girl you were, the teenager you became, and every version in between.

03 OUR STORY

And then... somehow, there was us.

Life has a strange way of introducing people to each other.

Sometimes you don't realise the importance of a person when they first enter your life.

You just talk. You laugh. You get comfortable. You slowly start knowing each other.

And then one day, you realise... "Oh. She's different."

04 YOU

Somewhere along the way, you became my person.

Not just someone I talk to.

Not just someone I love.

But someone whose presence slowly became part of my everyday life.

Tumse baat karna, tumhari random stories sunna, tumhari complaints, tumhari excitement... sab normal sa lagne laga.

And somehow, "normal" started feeling like home.

05 THE LITTLE THINGS

It's not only the big things I love about you.

It's the little things.

The way you talk. The way you react. The random things you say. The expressions you make. The little habits you probably don't even notice.

Kabhi tumhari ek chhoti si baat pura mood change kar deti hai.

Maybe that's what love really is noticing the little things and loving them anyway.

06 THE CHAOS

Of course... you're not always easy.

Let's be honest.

Kabhi mood swings. Kabhi unnecessary arguments. Kabhi "kuch nahi hua" jab clearly kuch hua hota hai.

Kabhi tum right. Kabhi main right. Aur kabhi... obviously main right.

Okay fine. Mostly tum hi right.

But I wouldn't trade the chaos for a quieter life.

07 COMFORT

With you, I don't always need words.

There's something beautiful about being comfortable enough to simply exist around someone.

No pretending. No trying too hard. No need to make every moment special.

Kabhi bas baat karni hoti hai. Kabhi bas sunna hota hai. Kabhi kuch bhi nahi.

And somehow, silence doesn't feel empty when it's with you.

08 THE LAUGHTER

You make ordinary days feel less ordinary.

Ek normal sa conversation, ek stupid joke, ek random topic... aur suddenly pura mood better.

Tumhare saath hasi ka reason hamesha logical hona zaroori nahi.

Sometimes we laugh because something is actually funny.

Sometimes we laugh because we're both baura.

And honestly, I love both versions.

09 MY SAFE PLACE

Somewhere along the way, you became a safe place.

A place where I can be myself.

Where I don't have to pretend that everything is always okay.

Where I can talk about random dreams, stupid thoughts, fears, plans, everything.

And knowing that someone will listen... matters more than you know.

10 OUR MEMORIES

I don't want just perfect memories with you.

I want the real ones too.

The random conversations. The silly fights. The late replies. The unexpected calls. The moments we couldn't stop laughing.

Because someday, these ordinary moments might become the memories we miss the most.

I don't want a perfect story.
I want our story.

11 CHOICE

Love isn't only a feeling.

Sometimes love is choosing someone again and again.

On the good days. On the difficult days. On the days when everything feels easy. And especially on the days when it doesn't.

And if life gives me the choice to keep choosing one person...

I hope I keep choosing you.

12 OUR FUTURE

There's still so much we haven't lived yet.

Abhi toh bahut kuch baaki hai.

New places. New experiences. New memories. New problems. New celebrations. New birthdays.

Kuch plans perfectly work karenge. Kuch completely fail honge.

But I don't need to know exactly what the future looks like.

I just hope I get to see it with you.

13 MY LIFE PARTNER

You're not just someone I love.

You're someone I imagine my life with.

The person I want beside me when things are exciting... and when things are difficult.

Someone with whom I want to celebrate the wins, survive the bad days, laugh at stupid things, and grow through everything life brings.

Mujhe future ki har detail nahi pata.

But one thing I know... I don't want my future to be just about me.

I want there to be an us.

14 YOUR BIRTHDAY

So today isn't just another birthday to me.

Today is the day I get to celebrate the person I love.

The person who somehow became such a big part of my little world.

And for one day, I want everything to stop for a moment.

No stress. No overthinking. No worries.

Just you. Your smile. And a day that's completely yours.

15 ONE WISH

If I could give you one thing...

I wouldn't give you a perfect life.

Because a perfect life doesn't exist.

I'd give you the strength to get through the difficult days, the courage to chase everything you dream about, and enough happiness to make all the difficult days worth it.

I hope you never feel like you have to become someone else to be loved.

Because the person you are right now...

is already the person I choose.

“

I don't know what the future will look like.

Shayad life humare liye bahut saare unexpected turns rakhti ho. Shayad sab kuch plan ke according na ho.

But whenever I imagine the person I want beside me through all those chapters...

somehow, it's always you.

2008 You were born.

YEARS LATER Our paths crossed.

Two completely different lives somehow became part of the same story.

THEN You became important.

Conversations became memories, and memories became something much bigger.

NOW You're my person.

The person I want to celebrate, annoy, support, laugh with and love.

NEXT Whatever comes next...

I hope we get to write that chapter together.

Before we move to the next chapter...

there's just one thing I want you to know.

I don't love you only for the best version of you.

I love the real you. The happy you. The irritated you. The sleepy you. The dramatic you. The stubborn you. The soft you.

All the little versions. All the imperfect moments. All the memories we've already made.

And all the ones we haven't made yet.

I'm really glad you were born.
